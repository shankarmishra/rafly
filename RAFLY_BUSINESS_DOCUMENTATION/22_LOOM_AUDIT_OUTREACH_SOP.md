## 1. 60-90 SECOND AUDIT STRUCTURE
- **0:00 - 0:15:** Introduction & genuine praise.
- **0:15 - 0:45:** Point out 2 specific technical bottlenecks (e.g. 4s mobile load delay, missing security header).
- **0:45 - 0:90:** Offer low-friction CTA ("Happy to share a 2-page fix guide if useful").
- **Rule:** Never say "Your website is bad." Say "I noticed 2 bottlenecks that might be costing you leads."
