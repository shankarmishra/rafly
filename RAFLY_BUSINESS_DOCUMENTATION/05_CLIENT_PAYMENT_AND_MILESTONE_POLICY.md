## 1. STANDARD MILESTONE STRUCTURE

To ensure mutual commitment and clear delivery checkpoints, all fixed-scope projects follow RAFly’s standardized 30/40/30 milestone structure:

```
[30% Kickoff Deposit] ──> [40% Staging Demo Milestone] ──> [30% Production Launch & Handover]
```

1. **30% Kickoff Deposit:** Payable prior to project initiation, requirements lock, and infrastructure provisioning.
2. **40% Staging Milestone:** Payable upon completion of the staging environment demo and completion of Round 1 revisions.
3. **30% Final Deployment Balance:** Payable prior to DNS cutover, production server launch, and transfer of administrative credentials.

---

## 2. INVOICING & PAYMENT TERMS

- **Invoice Issuance:** Invoices are issued digitally with 7-day payment terms from the date of milestone completion.
- **Accepted Payment Methods:** Bank NEFT/RTGS/IMPS, UPI, Corporate Credit Cards, Direct Gateway Links.
- **Third-Party Costs:** Domain registrations, web hosting fees, paid plugin licenses, and advertising spend (Meta/Google Ads) are paid directly by the Client or billed at actual cost without markup.

---

## 3. RETAINER COMMITMENT TERMS

### Managed Security & Growth Retainers
- **Minimum Commitment:** All monthly retainers require a **minimum 3-month commitment period**.
- **Renewal:** Automatically converts to a month-to-month retainer following the initial 3-month period.
- **Cancellation Notice:** 30 days written notice required prior to the next billing cycle.
- **Early Termination Fee:** Early cancellation during the initial 3-month commitment incurs a fee equal to 50% of the remaining contract value.

---

## 4. LATE PAYMENT POLICY & SERVICE SUSPENSION

| Days Past Due | Penalty & Action Required |
|---|---|
| **0 – 7 Days** | Grace Period — Standard payment reminder sent. No late fees. |
| **8 – 14 Days** | **5% Late Fee** automatically added to unpaid invoice balance. |
| **15 – 21 Days** | **Service Pause** — Development, staging access, and retainer SLAs suspended until full payment received. |
| **22+ Days** | **Formal Collections** — Account referred for legal collection and contract termination. |

- All late fees are non-negotiable. Service resumption requires settlement of full principal + late fees.
