## 1. CRM PIPELINE STAGES
`New Lead` $\rightarrow$ `Contacted` $\rightarrow$ `Qualified` $\rightarrow$ `Discovery Call` $\rightarrow$ `Proposal Sent` $\rightarrow$ `Negotiation` $\rightarrow$ `Won / Lost` $\rightarrow$ `Onboarding` $\rightarrow$ `Active` $\rightarrow$ `Retainer Expansion`.

## 2. REQUIRED LEAD DATA FIELDS
- `lead_id`, `company_name`, `contact_name`, `contact_email`, `contact_phone`, `service_interest`, `budget_bracket`, `urgency_level`, `qualification_score`, `lead_status`, `assigned_owner`.
