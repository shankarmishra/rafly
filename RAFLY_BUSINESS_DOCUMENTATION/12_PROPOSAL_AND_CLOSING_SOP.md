## 1. PROPOSAL PREPARATION PROTOCOL
1. Proposals must be generated within **24 hours** of the Discovery Call.
2. Always provide **Two (2) Investment Options** (e.g. Option A: Core Build vs Option B: Build + Growth Retainer).
3. Confirm SOW milestone terms (30% / 40% / 30%).

## 2. CLOSING & FOLLOW-UP SCHEDULE
- **Day 1:** Send Proposal + SOW via email and share link on WhatsApp.
- **Day 2:** 10-Minute Proposal Review Call to address questions.
- **Day 4:** First follow-up ("Any technical questions on the SOW scope?").
- **Day 7:** Final follow-up before closing pipeline entry.
