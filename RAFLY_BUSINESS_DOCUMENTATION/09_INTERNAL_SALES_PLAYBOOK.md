## 2. HANDLING COMMON CLIENT OBJECTIONS

### Objection 1: "Freelancer X is offering a website for ₹5,000."
- **Internal Positioning:** Do not compete on cheap pricing.
- **Scripted Response:** *"We completely understand there are cheap templates available. RAFly builds custom, secure digital growth systems with 100% asset ownership, speed guarantees (< 1.5s), and dedicated security SLAs. A ₹5k website often breaks, loads slowly, and has zero security support."*

### Objection 2: "Why can't you show fixed price cards on the website?"
- **Scripted Response:** *"Every business scope is unique. A 5-page brochure site has different infrastructure needs than an e-commerce platform. We scope during a free call so the quote we give is fixed and doesn't change later."*

### Objection 3: "Is ad revenue or sales guaranteed?"
- **Scripted Response:** *"We guarantee high-performing ad setup, targeted campaign optimization, and fast landing pages. We do not guarantee arbitrary sales numbers as sales depend on market demand, product offer, and fulfillment."*
