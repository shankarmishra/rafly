## 2. AUDIT BREAKDOWN BY CATEGORY

### A. Clearly Defined & Ready for Operationalization
1. **Core Identity & Positioning:** "Build. Protect. Grow." hierarchy (Build: Web/Store; Protect: Security/Recovery/Maintenance; Grow: Ads/CRO/Lead Automation).
2. **ICP Framework:** ICP-A (Local Professional Services), ICP-B (D2C E-commerce), ICP-C (Compromised Web Infrastructure).
3. **Disqualification Criteria:** Sub-₹15k budgets, contract-refusers, unlimited revision seekers, illegal/unethical niches.
4. **Milestone Payment Structure:** 30% Kickoff Deposit $\rightarrow$ 40% Staging Demo $\rightarrow$ 30% Final Deployment & Handover.
5. **Asset Ownership Policy:** 100% transfer of custom code, DNS, hosting, databases, ad accounts, and analytics upon 100% invoice settlement.
6. **Revision Governance:** 2 formal revision rounds per project scope; subsequent changes require a signed Change Request Form.

### B. Decision Required (`DECISION REQUIRED`)
1. `DECISION REQUIRED`: **Referral Reward Program Cap:** Define whether referral rewards are flat credits (e.g. ₹5,000 credit) vs percentage with cap (e.g. 10% capped at ₹10,000 per closed deal).
2. `DECISION REQUIRED`: **Retainer Commitment Period:** Establish whether standard security/growth retainers require a minimum 3-month contract commitment or run month-to-month.
3. `DECISION REQUIRED`: **Late Payment Grace Period:** Confirm whether late invoices (> 14 days) incur a 5% late fee or pause active maintenance services.

### C. Internal Targets vs Customer Promises (`INTERNAL TARGET — NOT CUSTOMER PROMISE`)
1. `INTERNAL TARGET — NOT CUSTOMER PROMISE`: **Average Deal Value Target:** ₹45,000 ($600).
2. `INTERNAL TARGET — NOT CUSTOMER PROMISE`: **Client Retainer Retention:** > 75% 6-month retention rate.
3. `INTERNAL TARGET — NOT CUSTOMER PROMISE`: **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) Growth:** Target ₹3,00,000/mo by Q4.
4. `INTERNAL TARGET — NOT CUSTOMER PROMISE`: **Outreach Conversion Rates:** Prospecting response rates and pipeline conversion metrics are operational benchmarks, not guarantees.

### D. Operational Capability Requirements (`OPERATIONAL CAPABILITY REQUIRED`)
1. `OPERATIONAL CAPABILITY REQUIRED`: **Enterprise Emergency Triage SLA (1 Hour):** Requires a dedicated 24/7 incident hotline and automated pager rotation.
2. `OPERATIONAL CAPABILITY REQUIRED`: **60-Second Lead Qualification:** Requires webhook integration between web forms, CRM, and WhatsApp API.

### E. Public vs Strictly Confidential Classification
- **CLIENT-FACING / PUBLIC:** Service descriptions, Build-Protect-Grow methodology, Asset ownership guarantee, Indicative investment ranges, Milestone structure (30/40/30), Client onboarding guide.
- **STRICTLY INTERNAL / CONFIDENTIAL:** Internal hourly rate (₹1,500-₹2,500/hr), complexity multipliers, lead scoring matrix, internal margin targets (>65%), sales objection handling scripts.
