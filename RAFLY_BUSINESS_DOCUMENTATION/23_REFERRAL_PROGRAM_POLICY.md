**RAFly Digital Growth**  
*Client & Partner Referral Reward Program*  

---

## 1. REFERRAL REWARD TERMS

RAFly offers a **10% referral bonus** of the total project contract value, capped at a maximum of **₹10,000 per successfully closed deal**.

### Eligibility Requirements
- The referred client must sign a formal Statement of Work (SOW) and pay the initial 30% kickoff deposit.
- The referrer must be a current/past RAFly client, vendor, or authorized business partner.
- The referral reward is processed within fourteen (14) days following receipt of the kickoff deposit.

### Disbursement Options
- **Billing Credit:** Applied as a discount credit toward your next RAFly project or monthly retainer invoice.
- **Direct Bank Transfer:** Paid directly to your registered bank account (TDS deducted as per applicable Indian Income Tax laws).

---

## 2. REFERRAL CLAIM PROCESS

1. **Submission:** Share your referral's name and contact details via email to `referrals@rafly.in` or directly to your Account Manager before kickoff.
2. **Verification:** Sales team logs `referral_source` in CRM upon lead qualification.
3. **Payout:** Reward is calculated and disbursed within 14 days of deposit confirmation.
