## 1. REFERRAL TRACKING WORKFLOW

```text
Referral Received ──> Log in CRM ("REFERRAL") ──> Lead Qualification ──> Deal Closed (Deposit Paid) ──> Reward Disbursement
```

1. **Logging Referral:** When a lead indicates they were referred by an existing client, set `lead_source = 'REFERRAL'` and tag `referrer_client_id` in CRM.
2. **Reward Calculation:** Calculate 10% of closed project contract value (capped at maximum ₹10,000).
3. **Approval:** Sales Lead verifies 30% kickoff deposit receipt and approves referral voucher in admin panel.
4. **Disbursement:** Account credit applied or bank transfer processed within fourteen (14) days of project kickoff.
