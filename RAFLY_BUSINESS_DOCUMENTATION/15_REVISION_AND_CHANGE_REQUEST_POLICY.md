## 1. REVISION POLICY RULES
- Every standard project SOW includes **two (2) formal revision rounds** during the Staging Phase.
- Revisions must be submitted in a single consolidated document within 5 business days of Staging Demo presentation.

## 2. CHANGE REQUEST PROCESS
- Any feature addition outside the SOW (e.g. new page, custom feature, extra integration) requires a signed **Change Request Form**.
- Change Request Rate: ₹1,500 / hour.
