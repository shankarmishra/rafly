# 11_DISCOVERY_CALL_SCRIPT.md — 15-Minute Discovery Call Script

**Goal:** Diagnose prospect pain points, confirm ICP fit, and establish scope direction.

---

## TIMELINE & QUESTION BREAKDOWN

### 0 – 2 Min: Business Context & Icebreaker
- *"Hi [Name], thanks for connecting. To make the best use of our 15 minutes, I’d love to understand your current digital setup and where you're looking to scale."*

### 2 – 5 Min: Problem Diagnosis
- *"What is the single biggest bottleneck with your current website/system right now? (Speed, leads, security, conversions?)"*

### 5 – 8 Min: Business Impact
- *"How is this issue affecting your daily leads or revenue? What happens if it stays unfixed for another 6 months?"*

### 8 – 11 Min: Desired Outcome & Timeline
- *"If we fix this, what does success look like for your business in 90 days? What is your target launch timeline?"*

### 11 – 13 Min: Recommended Direction & Budget Check
- *"Based on what you've shared, a custom Build + Lead Automation package would solve this. Our standard engagements for this scope range between ₹35k - ₹50k. Does that align with your planned investment?"*

### 13 – 15 Min: Clear Next Steps
- *"Great. I will prepare a tailored 2-option Proposal and SOW by tomorrow afternoon. Let's schedule 10 mins on Thursday to review it together."*
