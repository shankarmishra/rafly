## 1. YOUR PROJECT ROADMAP

```text
1. Kickoff Call & Access Lock ──> 2. Requirements & Content Lock ──> 3. Staging Build ──> 4. Review & Revisions ──> 5. Final Launch
```

---

## 2. WHAT WE NEED FROM YOU AT KICKOFF

To prevent project delays, please collect and share the following items during Week 1:

1. **Brand Assets:** Vector logos (.SVG / .PNG), brand color guidelines, and existing media assets.
2. **Access Credentials:** Domain registrar login, DNS manager access, current web hosting credentials (if applicable).
3. **Copy & Content:** Core company descriptions, team profiles, service details, and image assets.
4. **Designated Point of Contact:** Name and WhatsApp number of the single decision-maker authorized to approve milestones.

---

## 3. COMMUNICATION CHANNELS

- **Primary Communication:** Dedicated Client WhatsApp Group for quick daily updates.
- **Formal Milestone Approvals:** Email updates sent to your designated project lead.
- **Emergency Triage:** Direct hotline number for active production issues.
