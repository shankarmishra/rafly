## 2. Strict Anti-Doorway Quality Gate Policy

To protect RAFly from doorway-page penalties and search engine spam flags, every indexable location page MUST satisfy:

1. **Unique Local Context**: Real regional business environment description (e.g. Noida Sector 62 tech corridor vs Gurgaon Cyber City).
2. **Zero Fabricated Claims**: No fake office addresses, no fake local phone numbers, no fake local client reviews.
3. **100% Unique Copy**: Substantially distinct introductory prose, local business challenges, and regional FAQs.
4. **Contextual Internal Links**: Direct links to primary services (`/web-development`, `/web-security`, etc.) and neighboring hubs.
