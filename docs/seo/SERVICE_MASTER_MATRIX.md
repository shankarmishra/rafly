## 2. Standard 13-Section Service Page Structure

Every service page strictly implements the following high-converting, search-intent optimized structure:

1. **Hero Section**: Eyebrow, H1 title, lead description, primary CTA, and quick metrics.
2. **Core Capabilities**: What RAFly actually includes in this service.
3. **Signals / Problems Solved**: Concrete pain points and friction RAFly resolves.
4. **Deliverables**: Detailed list of tangible assets handed over.
5. **Process**: 4-step execution timeline with realistic timeframes.
6. **Technology Stack**: Technology tools and engines utilized (PHP, Laravel, Shopify, WAF, GTM).
7. **Ideal Audience / Fit**: Who this service is explicitly designed for.
8. **Explicit Boundaries**: What RAFly does NOT provide (transparency & risk prevention).
9. **Proof / Case Studies**: Relevant real system proof and work metrics.
10. **Frequently Asked Questions**: 4+ answered FAQs with schema markup (`FAQPage`).
11. **Related Services**: Cross-linking to adjacent service capabilities.
12. **Relevant Locations**: Contextual links to regional service hubs.
13. **Final CTA**: High-converting lead form card.
