# RAFly Team OS — Creative Studio & Reel Production Workflow

**Document Version:** 1.0.0  
**Target Output:** High-Converting Short Form Reels, Shorts, Ads, & Brand Creatives  

---

## 1. REEL PRODUCTION LIFECYCLE (9 STAGES)

```
Idea ──> Script ──> Assets ──> Editing ──> Internal Review ──> Client Review ──> Revision ──> Approved ──> Published
```

1. **Idea:** Concept logged with target audience, hook angle, and platform (Instagram/YouTube/Meta Ads).
2. **Script:** Script & storyboard written and approved by Marketing Lead.
3. **Asset Assembly:** Raw footage, SFX, voiceovers, and logos uploaded to Creative Vault.
4. **Editing:** Reel Editor assigned, rough cut prepared.
5. **Internal Review:** Creative Director reviews and leaves timestamped annotations.
6. **Client Review:** Client receives time-limited preview link in Client Portal.
7. **Revision:** Editor addresses feedback (Max 2 revision rounds).
8. **Approved:** Client / Manager marks final approval stamp.
9. **Published:** Scheduled and posted; analytics tracked.

---

## 2. TIMESTAMPED ANNOTATION SYSTEM

Comments on video assets attach directly to specific time markers:
- `00:07`: *"Subtitle font size is too small; increase by 20%."*
- `00:14`: *"Use official RAFly secondary blue `#2563EB` here."*
- `00:22`: *"Trim this 1-second pause before CTA."*
